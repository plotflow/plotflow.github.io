Original work

A limited number of commissions are taken each year. Every commissioned piece is an original design: a machine designed for the sheet, in the studio's low-poly line-work style, and it exists nowhere else. One drawing, one owner.

Copyrighted characters and designs are not accepted. The Universal-Century sheets on this site are studies; they are displayed, not sold or reproduced.

Scope

  • Original mecha: a machine designed with you (role, silhouette, and detail brief welcome)
  • Other subjects: ships, aircraft, engines, architecture, anything that rewards line work
  • Sizes: standard 11×14″ or larger formats
  • Ink and paper: other pen types and paper stocks by arrangement

Process

  1. Inquiry: email the brief: subject, references for mood (not for copying), preferred size
  2. Quote: pricing, timeline, and feasibility notes follow by reply
  3. Design: the machine is designed and rebuilt as vector line work
  4. Approval: a digital preview of the paths is reviewed before plotting
  5. Plot: the sheet is drawn, inspected, signed, and shipped

Pricing

Pricing depends on subject complexity, size, and turnaround. Custom pieces typically start at $350 for 11×14″. A firm quote is given before any work begins.
