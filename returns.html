Condition on arrival

Every sheet is inspected before it leaves the studio. A copy that arrives damaged is replaced at no cost.

Damaged in transit

Report damage within 7 days of delivery, with photographs of the sheet and the packaging. A replacement is plotted and shipped once the claim is confirmed.

  • Email hello@plotflow.io with the order number and photos
  • Keep the original packaging until the claim is resolved

Made to order

Each copy is plotted after the order is placed, so returns for change of mind are not accepted. Once the plotter has run, the sheet exists; it cannot be undrawn.

Cancellations

An order can be cancelled within 24 hours of purchase, provided plotting has not begun. Email as soon as possible with the order number.
