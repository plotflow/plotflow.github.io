PLOTFLOW* — pen-plotter studio

This studio makes drawings of Universal-Century mobile suits with a pen plotter. A machine moves a 0.3 mm pigment fineliner across Strathmore Bristol, stroke by stroke, following a prepared program. A typical sheet takes twenty to thirty minutes of continuous drawing and ten to thirty metres of ink.

Because ink flow depends on temperature, humidity, and feed rate, no two copies of an edition are identical. Each is inspected, signed, and numbered. Editions are limited to 25.

The live plot — a real-time simulation of the machine at work — is on the console page.

Orders are plotted after they are placed; allow two to three weeks before dispatch. Shipping is free within the U.S. and a flat $20 elsewhere. Sheets damaged in transit are replaced.

New editions are released irregularly. To be notified when one is released or restocked, leave an email address.
