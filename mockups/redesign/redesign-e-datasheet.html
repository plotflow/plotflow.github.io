1.0Scope

PLOTFLOW produces pen-plotted drawings of Universal-Century mobile suits. Each copy is drawn individually by machine, in ink, on archival paper. This document describes the current editions, the drawing method, and how to order.

2.0Method

2.1 — The subject is rebuilt as vector line work and compiled to a stroke program.

2.2 — An AxiDraw plotter executes the program with a Staedtler Triplus 0.3 mm pigment fineliner on Strathmore Bristol 300, 11×14 in. Pen-down feed is 1100 mm/min.

2.3 — Ink laydown varies with temperature, humidity, and feed. Copies within an edition therefore differ in line weight and saturation. No correction is applied.

2.4 — Finished sheets are inspected, signed, and numbered by hand.

3.0Current editions

DesignationCode Ink, mStrokesVertices Plot timeAvailability

Plot time includes pen-up travel (6600 mm/min) and 0.10 s per pen lift. Values are computed from the stroke program, not estimated.

5.0Ordering

  1. Each copy is plotted after the order is placed. Allow 2–3 weeks before dispatch.
  2. Shipping: free within the U.S.; flat $20 international. Taxes at checkout.
  3. Copies damaged in transit are replaced at no cost.
  4. To be notified when an edition is released or restocked, leave an email address. It is used for that purpose only.
