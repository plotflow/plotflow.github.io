私たちについて

Drawn by machine.

PLOTFLOW is an independent studio. It makes drawings of Universal-Century mobile suits with a pen plotter — a machine that holds a pen and draws, one stroke at a time, following a prepared program. Every mark on every sheet was made by a pen.

Each edition begins as vector line work, rebuilt from reference path by path and compiled to a stroke program. An AxiDraw executes the program with a Staedtler Triplus 0.3 mm pigment fineliner on Strathmore 300 Bristol. A typical sheet takes twenty to thirty minutes of continuous drawing and ten to thirty metres of ink.

Ink flow depends on temperature, humidity, and feed rate, so no two copies of an edition are identical, even from the same path data. The variation is not corrected. It is the record of a particular machine drawing a particular sheet on a particular day.

Editions are limited to 25. Each sheet is inspected, signed, and numbered by hand.
