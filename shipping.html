Domestic (U.S.)

Shipping within the U.S. is free and included in the price. Each sheet ships flat, with tracking.

  • Free standard shipping on every commissioned piece
  • Typical delivery: 2 – 5 business days after dispatch

International

Canada, the U.K., Australia, Germany, France, and Japan: flat $20, selected at checkout. International orders go by tracked carrier.

  • Flat $20 international shipping
  • Typical delivery: 1 – 4 weeks, depending on destination and customs
  • Customs duties and import taxes are the buyer's responsibility

Made to order

Each commissioned piece is drawn after the design is approved. Allow 2 – 3 weeks for production before dispatch. A tracking number is sent when the order ships.

Packaging

Each sheet is sleeved, faced with glassine to protect the ink, sandwiched between rigid boards, and sealed in a stay-flat mailer.
